Overview

Supported the development and launch of a new manufacturing line for a next-generation AI data center cooling system through a six-month New Product Introduction (NPI) program.

As a Manufacturing Engineering Intern, I worked with design, manufacturing, and production teams to support assembly line development, engineering documentation, process implementation, production readiness, and early manufacturing support.

The project provided hands-on experience with the transition from engineering design to a production-ready manufacturing system. Technical product details and proprietary manufacturing information are intentionally omitted.

Problem / Goals / Requirements

Problem

  • A new AI cooling product required a dedicated manufacturing line.
  • Product and manufacturing requirements changed throughout development.
  • Engineering and production teams needed to stay aligned during launch.
  • The process required validation before full production.

Goals

  • Support a production-ready assembly system.
  • Help turn product design into a repeatable manufacturing process.
  • Improve documentation clarity and launch readiness.
  • Support early manufacturing operations.

Requirements

  • Maintain accurate engineering documentation and revision control.
  • Align product design with manufacturing capabilities.
  • Support repeatable assembly processes.
  • Keep communication clear across engineering and production.
  • Validate production readiness before launch.

System / Architecture

The manufacturing system used coordinated assembly operations to transform components into completed AI data center cooling systems. Development included assembly sequencing, production workflows, engineering documentation, material movement, validation, and production support.

As product designs evolved, the manufacturing process was refined through engineering revisions and production feedback. The workflow connected product development, process planning, line implementation, validation, and launch support into one NPI cycle.
