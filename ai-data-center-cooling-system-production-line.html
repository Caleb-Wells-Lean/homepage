Overview

This project involved the development and launch of a new manufacturing line for a cooling system designed for AI data center applications. The work progressed over approximately six months from product design and manufacturing planning through line development, production readiness, and initial production.

As a Manufacturing Engineering Intern, I supported engineers and production personnel throughout multiple stages of the project. My contributions included engineering support, manufacturing documentation, assembly line development, production preparation, issue resolution, and collaboration between design and manufacturing teams.

The project provided direct experience with the complete transition from engineering design to a functioning production process. Because this was an active industrial program, the case study remains intentionally high-level and excludes proprietary technical and production information.

System / Architecture

The manufacturing system was developed as a sequence of coordinated assembly operations that transformed components and subassemblies into a completed cooling product. The line required defined work areas, assembly sequencing, material movement, engineering documentation, production support, and validation activities.

As the product design matured, the manufacturing process was refined alongside it. Engineering changes and production feedback were incorporated into the line so that the assembly process could progress from an initial development state into a repeatable production system.

The final manufacturing architecture connected product design, process planning, line implementation, validation, and production support into one continuous development cycle.
