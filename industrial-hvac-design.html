Overview

Supported the engineering and production of custom industrial HVAC systems by updating CAD models, revising manufacturing documentation, and assisting with design-to-production execution. Collaborated with engineering and manufacturing teams to ensure drawings, models, and build documentation remained accurate throughout production.

Working on highly customized equipment strengthened my experience in production engineering, manufacturability, documentation control, and cross-functional problem solving within a fast-paced manufacturing environment.

System / Architecture

Custom HVAC systems combined structural, mechanical, airflow, and electrical assemblies into configurable customer-specific products. CAD models and engineering drawings served as the primary link between design and manufacturing, requiring accurate revision control and continuous coordination with production throughout the build process.
